═ is my app leaking? ═

Built your app with Lovable, Bolt, v0, Replit or Emergent? Paste its address. In about ten seconds you get a grade, what each problem means in plain words, and a prompt to paste back into your builder that fixes it.

$

Crumple reads only what your site sends to every visitor: the page and its JavaScript. It never signs in, never touches your database and keeps nothing about you. Results are cached for ten minutes.

═ go deeper ═

The web check can't look inside your database, because it can't know the app is yours. On your own computer it can: it counts which Supabase tables, or which Firebase data, anyone on the internet can read, without reading the data itself.

$ npx crumplelab site myapp.lovable.app --mine

Needs Node.js. Free and open source: github.com/priberry-data/crumplelab
